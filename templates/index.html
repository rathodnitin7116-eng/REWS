Understanding ransomware attack vectors, MITRE ATT&CK T1486, and heuristic early warning mechanisms.

MITRE ATT&CK FRAMEWORK // T1486

How Ransomware Works & Why REWS Matters

Ransomware is malicious software designed to deny access to computer systems and personal files by encrypting them with military-grade cryptography (AES-256, ChaCha20, RSA-4096) and demanding an extortion payment in cryptocurrency. Traditional signature-based antivirus frequently misses novel or zero-day ransomware. REWS provides an essential defensive layer: Behavioral & Heuristic Early Warning.

🛡️
01

Rapid Mutation Bursts

Ransomware engines automate bulk encryption, altering dozens or hundreds of files within seconds. REWS tracks the velocity of file alterations; when change rates exceed the burst threshold, an immediate Critical Alert is triggered.

02

Suspicious Extensions

Strains such as LockBit, WannaCry, and BlackCat append recognizable or double suffixes (.locked, .crypt, .docx.locked). REWS inspects filename patterns and alerts operators instantly.

03

Extortion Ransom Notes

Attackers drop text or HTML files instructing victims on ransom payments (HOW_TO_RESTORE_FILES.txt). REWS monitors regex patterns to catch and flag ransom note creation before widespread encryption completes.

04

Cryptographic SHA-256 Baselines

By computing cryptographic SHA-256 digests, REWS maintains an immutable baseline of monitored assets. Any unauthorized bit flips or mass encryptions immediately alter the cryptographic digest.

05

Defense-in-Depth & 3-2-1 Backups

Early warning is vital, but recovery demands resilience. Organizations must maintain the 3-2-1 backup rule: 3 copies of data, 2 different media types, and 1 immutable or offline air-gapped copy immune to network ransomware.

06

Incident Response Procedures

Upon alert confirmation: 1. Isolate infected host from local network immediately. 2. Preserve volatile memory and audit logs for digital forensics. 3. Engage security responders and never pay ransoms without expert guidance.

Academic Scope Disclaimer: REWS is an educational host-based heuristic monitoring prototype. It observes explicitly targeted directories, calculates hashes, and flags behavioral anomalies. It does not replace full-featured enterprise EDR agents or antivirus software.
